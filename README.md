# AI in Healthcare: Course Portfolio

# Overview
This portfolio presents the complete body of work completed for ITAI 4375: Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare at Houston Community College. The course explores the rapidly evolving intersection of artificial intelligence and modern healthcare, emphasizing both foundational knowledge and practical application. Through a sequence of structured modules, students examine how artificial intelligence supports diagnostics, clinical decision making, population health analytics, and responsible data science. The portfolio demonstrates mastery of key concepts through written assignments, case analyses, critiques of current research, and the design of artificial intelligence driven healthcare scenarios.

# Course Context
Artificial intelligence has become one of the most transformative forces in healthcare. Hospitals, research institutions, and health technology companies increasingly rely on artificial intelligence to improve patient care, automate workflows, enhance diagnostic accuracy, and support clinical decision making. This course provides a comprehensive introduction to these developments, beginning with artificial intelligence fundamentals and progressing toward advanced applications such as natural language processing, reinforcement learning, and multimodal diagnostic systems. The course also emphasizes ethical considerations, fairness, and responsible data governance, ensuring that students understand both the capabilities and limitations of artificial intelligence in clinical environments.

# Course Structure
The course is delivered in an Online Anytime format, allowing students to complete work independently while adhering to structured deadlines. The curriculum includes lectures, hands on assignments, weekly discussions, and a midterm glossary project. Each module builds upon the previous one, creating a progressive learning pathway that moves from foundational concepts to advanced artificial intelligence applications.

The major modules include
* Re-visiting Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning Introduction to Medical Terminology
* Intorduction to Healthcare Data
* Introduction to Clinical Decision Support Systems
* AI-based Diagnostic Tools
* AI Risk Modelling and Prognosis
* Population Health Analytics using AI
* Responsible Data Science in Healthcare
* Natural Language Processing in Healthcare
* Reinforcement Learning in Healthcare
* Human-Centered AI Design (HCD) in healthcare
* Healthcare Conversational Agents
* Federated Learning in Healthcare
* Ethics and Cybersecurity in Healthcare AI
* The Future of AI in Healthcare

Each assignment in this portfolio corresponds to one of these modules and demonstrates applied understanding of the concepts introduced.

# Learning Objectives
The portfolio reflects the course’s core learning objectives, including the ability to
* Analyze and interpret healthcare data using artificial intelligence principles
* Evaluate diagnostic and prognostic tools using evidence based methods
* Critique artificial intelligence applications such as natural language processing and reinforcement learning
* Identify risks in clinical case studies and propose artificial intelligence supported mitigation strategies
* Explain artificial intelligence terminology clearly and accurately
* Apply ethical and responsible data science principles to artificial intelligence in healthcare
* Communicate complex concepts effectively through structured academic writing

These objectives align with the HCC Core Curriculum requirements for critical thinking, communication, and quantitative literacy.

# Portfolio Components
The portfolio includes a series of assignments that demonstrate progressive mastery of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

## Medical Terminology Identification
Introduces foundational medical language and reinforces the importance of precise terminology for artificial intelligence model development and clinical communication.

## Healthcare Dataset Analysis
Applies quantitative methods to mock clinical data, demonstrating the ability to compute descriptive statistics and interpret numerical variables in a healthcare context.

## Clinical Decision Support System Scenario
Explores future artificial intelligence powered decision support systems, analyzing capabilities, clinician interaction, benefits, and ethical considerations.

## Diagnostic Test Evaluation
Evaluates a modern diagnostic test using sensitivity, specificity, and evidence based reasoning, connecting traditional diagnostics to artificial intelligence driven tools.

## Prognostic Model Critique
Assesses a peer reviewed prognostic model, examining predictive factors, performance metrics, strengths, limitations, and clinical relevance.

## Non Traditional Health Data Analysis
Explores genetic testing as a form of non traditional health data, evaluating its benefits, risks, and implications for personalized medicine and population health.

## Clinical Risk Identification
Analyzes a real medical case study, identifies major clinical risks, and explains how artificial intelligence can support early detection and risk mitigation.

## Natural Language Processing Critique
Evaluates a natural language processing application in electronic health records, identifying benefits, limitations, and ethical considerations.

## Reinforcement Learning Scenario Design
Designs a reinforcement learning system for automated oxygen titration, defining states, actions, rewards, and potential clinical impact.

## Midterm Glossary
Presents an extensive glossary of artificial intelligence terminology, including definitions, examples, and significance statements for each term. This glossary supports interdisciplinary communication and conceptual clarity.

# Significance of the Portfolio
This portfolio demonstrates the ability to analyze, critique, and design artificial intelligence applications across a wide range of healthcare contexts. It reflects strong academic writing, evidence based reasoning, and a deep understanding of both the technical and clinical dimensions of artificial intelligence. The work also highlights the importance of responsible data science, ethical considerations, and interdisciplinary collaboration in the development and deployment of artificial intelligence systems.

The portfolio serves as a professional artifact that showcases readiness for roles in health technology, data science, clinical informatics, and artificial intelligence policy. It also provides a foundation for future study in advanced artificial intelligence, machine learning, and healthcare innovation.
