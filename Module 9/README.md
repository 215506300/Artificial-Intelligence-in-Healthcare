# Challenges and Insights
The analysis identifies several challenges associated with reinforcement learning in healthcare.
* Reinforcement learning requires large, high quality datasets that accurately represent clinical variability.
* Interpretability remains a major concern, as clinicians must understand the rationale behind algorithmic recommendations.
* Safety validation is essential to prevent harmful actions in real world settings.
* Reinforcement learning policies may fail to generalize across diverse patient populations.

These insights highlight the importance of responsible development, continuous monitoring, and clinician oversight.
