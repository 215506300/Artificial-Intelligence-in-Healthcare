# Assignment 7: Identifying Risks in a Medical Case Study

# Overview
This assignment analyzes a real medical case study published in JAMA and identifies three major health risks for a seventy two year old patient diagnosed with non ST elevation myocardial infarction. The work evaluates the risks of myocardial infarction progression, heart failure exacerbation, and medication related complications. Each risk is supported by recent peer reviewed research and clinical evidence. The assignment also discusses how artificial intelligence technologies can support early detection, risk stratification, and clinical decision making. This aligns with Module 7, which focuses on responsible data science, risk identification, and the role of AI in improving patient safety.

# Objectives
This assignment fulfills several key learning objectives of the course.
It demonstrates the ability to
* Summarize a clinical case study clearly and accurately
* Identify and explain major health risks relevant to the patient’s condition
* Support each identified risk with recent peer reviewed evidence
* Describe how AI technologies can mitigate or manage these risks
* Present a structured, academically rigorous analysis with proper APA citations

# Assignment Description
The task required selecting a medical case study from a reputable source and writing a report that includes a case summary, three potential health risks, explanations of why each risk is important, and a discussion of how AI can help manage these risks. The chosen case study describes a seventy two year old man presenting with chest pain, dyspnea, and elevated cardiac biomarkers, ultimately diagnosed with non ST elevation myocardial infarction. The assignment addressed the following components.
* A clear summary of the patient’s symptoms, history, and diagnosis
* Identification of three clinically significant risks
* Explanation of consequences if each risk is not addressed
* Discussion of AI based strategies for risk reduction
* A concluding reflection on the importance of risk identification

The completed work includes a title page, abstract, structured sections, and a full APA reference list.

# Methods and Approach
The assignment followed a structured analytical approach.
* A peer reviewed case study from JAMA was selected to ensure clinical accuracy.
* The patient’s age, comorbidities, symptoms, and diagnostic findings were analyzed to identify relevant risks.
* Recent literature from high impact journals was reviewed to support each risk with evidence.
* AI applications such as ECG interpretation, predictive modeling, and medication safety tools were examined to determine how they could mitigate the identified risks.
* The analysis was organized into clear sections to meet academic and formatting requirements.

This approach ensured clarity, accuracy, and strong alignment with the assignment’s expectations.

# Summary of Key Content
The assignment provides a comprehensive analysis of the patient’s condition and associated risks. Key points include
* The patient presented with chest pain radiating to the left arm, shortness of breath, nausea, diaphoresis, elevated troponin levels, and ST segment depression.
* Three major risks were identified: progression of myocardial infarction, heart failure exacerbation, and medication related complications.
* Each risk was supported by recent evidence, including mortality statistics, heart failure progression rates, and medication related readmission data.
* AI technologies were discussed as tools for early detection, risk prediction, and medication safety.
* The analysis emphasized the importance of timely intervention and responsible use of AI in clinical care.

# Results and Interpretation
The completed assignment demonstrates strong understanding of clinical risk identification and the role of AI in modern healthcare. # Challenges and Insights
The analysis identifies several challenges associated with managing risks in older adults.
* Delays in treatment significantly increase mortality in myocardial infarction.
* Heart failure progression is common after myocardial injury and requires close monitoring.
* Medication related complications are frequent in older adults with multiple comorbidities.
* AI systems must be used responsibly to avoid bias, misinterpretation, or overreliance.

These insights highlight the importance of combining clinical expertise with AI driven tools to improve patient safety.
